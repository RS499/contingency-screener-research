## 4. What must change
- The comparison at a matched **target** (c) is unfair: at 0.96 ridge escalates 71.4% and histgb 57.0% — ridge gets
  14 more points of solver budget. The operator pays in escalation, so compare missed rate at matched **escalation**.
- Content of the replacement claim (stored labels, from `data/sts_matched_escalation.json`): histgb misses fewer
  at 25–50% escalation, the two are indistinguishable from 51% to 70% — which includes both headline points
  (63.7% / 64.3%) — and ridge misses fewer only at 71–73%, just below ridge's escalation ceiling (≈ 75%). At 74–75%
  the comparison is incomplete (not every ridge split reaches that escalation); say nothing there. So neither model
  is uniformly safer; accuracy does not settle it, but the reason is the trade-off shape, not that the accurate model
  is unsafe.
- Heading (a): must not state a universal ordering. Content: the ranking depends on the solver budget.
- (b): "not safer at any point" is false at matched escalation (histgb safer at 25–50%).
- (d): true only at matched target; remove or scope.
- **Label caveat (N2):** on corrected labels (no retrain) the near-operating-point tie shifts: histgb@0.97 0.46 ±
  0.08% vs ridge@0.94 1.14 ± 0.57% (gap 0.68 > 0.57 → histgb lower; escalations 63.7% vs 64.3% are matched). So the
  ordering at the operating point is itself label-dependent; the text may not declare either model safer there
  without naming the label set (`P-003.md`).
- Optional figure (d-figs, new file + manifest): missed rate vs escalation for both models with ±1 std bands from
  `data/tuned_metrics.json` m2 sweeps (+0.45 pp). The existing Fig. 2 plots against target and cannot show this.
- (e) "accurately": ridge's MAE (3.754 × 10⁻³) is only 11.6% below persistence (4.246 × 10⁻³); scope the adverb or drop
  it (P-027).
- Depth (where the models differ most): `E9.md`.
