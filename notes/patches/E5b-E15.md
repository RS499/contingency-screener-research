## 4. What must change
**E5b (add, one sentence in words, after a):**
- The guarantee is on **marginal coverage over all contingencies**; it says nothing directly about the missed rate,
  which is measured, not guaranteed.
- The one thing the guarantee does imply: a certified violation requires the true voltage to fall below the band's
  lower edge, so the share of *all* contingencies that are certified violations is at most 1 − target. Divided by the
  violation rate, that bounds the missed rate only loosely (≈ 57% at 0.90, ≈ 17% at 0.97) — put these bound values in
  a footnote or table note (D15), not the main sentence.
- On violations specifically, the band misses often: at 0.90 the overshoot exceeds q̂ on ~39% (ridge) / ~41%
  (histgb) of violations. This is optional context (one clause) and label-dependent (P-003).
- (a) itself (P-010): the exchangeability condition is on **base cases** (each base case contributes 186 correlated
  outages; splits are by base case), not "a single-element outage". Fix in the same edit.
