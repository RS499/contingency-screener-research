## 4. What must change
- The same 24.93% of the data is called "without any equipment outages" (l.107, by definition of N-0),
  "two elements out" (l.111) and "still N-1" (l.367). One definition, stated **in Method** (the l.119
  paragraph is the natural home), used everywhere.
- The definition must carry: (a) the generator outage is part of the **base case** (drawn before the N-0
  acceptance check, never the slack, probability 0.30); (b) the contingency is then a single branch
  outage on that base case; (c) **no redispatch** — when a unit is out its active power is simply lost
  and the slack bus picks up the mismatch (POWER-11: closer to a G-1 + L-1 sequence than a clean N-1);
  (d) the surrogate is told which unit is out (per-generator on/off inputs), so these rows are not
  hidden from the model.
- The N-0 definition at l.107 must then be consistent with (a): either N-0 allows a unit on
  maintenance, or the text must say the base case may already have one unit out.
- l.111 and l.367 each shrink to a back-reference to the Method definition (or are cut); the Discussion
  sentence at l.367 must no longer be the first place the reader learns how these rows are classified.
- N-2 is defined twice as "two branches fail together" (l.132, l.367). Keep that definition and make the
  gen + branch rows explicitly distinct from it, so the reader does not read the 24.93% as N-2 data.
- l.119 "Since weaker grids failed the check more often" is a causal claim about rejection by outage
  status that the repo **cannot** support: rejected draws are not recorded (see §5). The author either
  drops the causal clause or scopes it to what is measured (accepted share 24.93% vs coded 30%).
- Optional (author-decision, not required for the fix): whether headline metrics differ between the
  gen-out and no-gen-out strata. **No committed key exists**; it would be a cheap analysis of existing
  predictions (`data/sts_n1_predictions_long.parquet`) by d-runs. Do not assert either way without it.
