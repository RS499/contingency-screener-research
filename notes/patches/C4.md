## 4. What must change (content only)
1. **One word per concept, used everywhere**, including captions, table headers and the Fig. 2 axis. The author picks the words. Three concepts need three distinct names:
   (a) the knob (S3);
   (b) the measured per-case coverage (S1, the "Cov." column);
   (c) the missed-violation rate (already has a consistent name).
   Bare "at 90% coverage" (#1, 3, 10, 26, 29, 31, 32, 40) must name the knob unambiguously.
2. **S2: drop it or rename it.** The paper never uses it after l.231. Options:
   (i) keep the selective-prediction citation (geifman2017, elyaniv2010) but describe the curve without the word "coverage";
   (ii) cut the parenthetical.
   Either way, OS-4's clause cannot survive in its current form: it redefines the word already defined at l.128.
3. **Resolve the two unparseable or mixed uses** (#7 at l.128, #8 at l.132). Say what the quantile level is set by (the knob), and what "single" means (one global quantile for all cases, per prior-art §6.3).
4. **Rename the "target" collisions** (#4 prediction variable; #5 load window; #36 held-out network), or at least #5 and #36, so that "target" means only the knob. This is optional but cheap.
5. **Add one explanation linking knob → measured coverage → missed-violation rate.** Place it at the first point where both numbers appear (l.199) or in III-C next to the S1 definition. It must carry these points:
   - The knob sets q̂. Measured coverage lands at the knob, which shows the band is calibrated.
   - The missed-violation rate is **not** 1 − coverage. Coverage is averaged over *all* cases. The missed rate is the share of *true violations* that the gate *certifies*.
   - A violation is missed only when it is certified, i.e. the band sits wholly above the limit. A band that is merely wrong for a case whose prediction lands in the escalate or flag region costs no miss.
   - Therefore a knob of 0.90 does not mean 10% of violations are missed. The measured values are the printed 4.72% (histgb) and 2.96% (ridge).
   - The missed rate is measured, not guaranteed (E5b; say this in words).
6. **Per D15, the numeric joint bound goes in a footnote or table, not the main text.** The bound: P(certify ∧ violation) ≤ α, so the conditional missed rate is ≤ α / P(violation). If the author includes it at all, it goes in a footnote (coordinate with E5b so it appears once).
7. **Table 2 header "Cov."** Expand it or make it match the chosen S1 word. "Target" must match the chosen S3 word.
8. **Fig. 2 caption vs axis.** The caption's "safety target" must equal the axis label, or both change (see §7).

- That measured coverage "above/below" the knob differs meaningfully when the gap is under one std.
