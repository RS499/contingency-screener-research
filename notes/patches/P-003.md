## 4. What must change
- **Method (at b):** state the oracle precisely — pandapower Newton–Raphson with Q limits enforced by one-way PV→PQ
  switching (a generator that hits a limit is never re-checked, even when its voltage ends on the wrong side of its
  setpoint) — and that **every label, violation rate and missed rate in the paper is measured against these
  labels**.
- **Audit result (Method or Discussion; one short paragraph or 2–3 sentences):** the author re-solved every row
  with a switch-back loop that releases wrongly pinned generators. Content: almost every solve (93.5% of rows) leaves
  at least one generator pinned on the wrong side; 9.5% of stored violations become safe and 0.9% of safe rows
  become violations; the overall violation rate moves 17.48% → 16.60% and the strip share 56.86% → 56.22% (the
  boundary-mass measurement is robust); the dataset minimum moves 0.7179 → 0.8077 pu. The featured 0.8485 pu case
  becomes safe (`P-001.md`).
- **Effect on the headline (must be reported, not buried):** applying the *same trained models* to the corrected
  labels (no retraining), histgb@0.97 misses 0.46 ± 0.08% (all 5 splits < 1%) instead of 0.83 ± 0.24%, while
  ridge@0.94 misses 1.14 ± 0.57% (3 of 5 splits > 1%) instead of 0.79 ± 0.21%. The histgb drop passes the std rule;
  the ridge rise does not. Either way the "which model is safer" reading depends on the label convention
  (`C8.md`, `Top5-2d-E5.md`).
- **Caveats carried with the audit (from the artifact):** models not retrained and the dataset not rebuilt on
  corrected labels; the switch-back loop is one standard PV/PQ logic (another switching order could reach a different
  consistent solution); N-0 acceptance decisions and N-0 input features were not corrected (9 accepted bases would
  fall below 0.94 under the corrected solve); no independent AC solver was run.
- (a): either give the corrected range beside the stored one, or caveat 0.7179 as a pandapower-label value.
- (d)/(e): depths are stored-label depths; say so (Fig. 3 rebuild handled in `P-001.md`/`E9.md`).
- (c): S_mean is cut (`E5b-E15.md`); do not replace it with S_p99.
- (f): 0.54% below 0.87 pu is a stored-label share (corrected minimum is 0.8077, so part of that tail is artifact).
- (g): add the label convention to the limitation list; the same sentence also carries the "dynamic stability"
  mix-up (`P-001.md` §4).
- **Framing (author's choice):** this is the second artifact the project found in its own data pipeline (after the
  setpoint clip, l.121). If the audit was prompted by the AI review panel, the disclosure (P-002) must say so.

## 6. Must not claim
- That the corrected labels are "true" voltages: one switching logic, same solver family, no independent AC solver.
- That the corrected missed rates are the pipeline's result (models were not retrained on corrected labels).
- That ridge becomes *less* safe under corrected labels (fails std rule) — only that its sub-1% reading does not
  hold on 3 of 5 splits.
- That the flips go one way.
