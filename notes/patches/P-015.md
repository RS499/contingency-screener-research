## 4. What must change
- l.119 says the published 30-bus run "is discussed in Section V". Section V gives only its base-case
  loading (the reason it was replaced), never a result from it. A reader infers a result was dropped
  (NS-12). The author picks one:
  - **Option A — drop the promise.** Keep only the reason the published case was replaced (its base
    case exceeds 100% line loading), in Method, and say its gate results are not reported because the
    base case is thermally infeasible. Cheapest; consistent with "report exclusions with a reason".
  - **Option B — report it once.** Give the published-case30 boundary mass and one operating point next
    to the regenerated case (numbers §5). This adds a third data point to the boundary-mass story
    (20.01% sits between 7.09% and 56.86%). If chosen, it must carry the same metrics as the other two
    networks and the same operating-point rule, and its thermal infeasibility must be stated beside it.
- Either way, the name must change with P-018: the pandapower `case30` is the PYPOWER "Washington 30 Bus
  Dynamic Test Case", not the IEEE 30-bus power-flow case (`case_ieee30`). "Published IEEE 30-bus" is
  therefore inaccurate for both the published and the regenerated runs, and the abstract's "IEEE 30-bus"
  (l.81) with them.
- The 111.83% appears twice (l.119, l.365). After the fix it appears once.
