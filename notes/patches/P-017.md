4. One name per concept. The collisions to resolve are:
   - coverage (C4);
   - target (C4 rows #4, #5, #36);
   - band vs boundary band vs strip;
   - floor, which means both the voltage limit and the escalation floor;
   - seeds vs splits;
   - variables vs hyperparameters.

## 6. Must not claim
- No definition may carry a claim the evidence does not support. In particular:
  - "boundary mass" must not be defined as a property of the network (Top-5 #1, P-007, D8);
  - the "floor" must not be called something the gate "must" have (Top-5 #2(b)).
- Glossing "exchangeable" must not imply that the guarantee holds per contingency or per base case (P-010).
- The DC gloss must not claim that DC methods cannot screen voltage at all (C5 / E6 concern a classical screen).
