## 4. What must change
The mechanism text (c) is wrong **in both branches**: the repo's own `data/miss_mechanism.json` shows the weak-bus
generator (gen 21, IEEE bus 54) pinned at its **absorbing** limit (Q = Q_min) while its voltage is ~0.106 pu *below*
its setpoint. A generator below setpoint should be producing reactive power, not absorbing it. "Reaches the limit of
reactive power and can no longer control the voltage" describes the opposite (a producing limit). So (c) and (d)
must be removed or rebuilt from the data regardless of N2.

**Branch 1 — N2 confirms** (`data/sts_n2_label_audit.json` shows the featured case, scenario 101000025 / line 78,
re-solving at or above 0.94 under a consistent PV/PQ back-off, and/or a non-negligible full-set flip share):
- Remove (a) as a statement about a real under-voltage event. If a deepest miss is still reported, it must be the
  deepest miss **on audited labels** at the stated operating point, read from the N2 artifact (not from
  `missed_depth.json`, which uses the unaudited labels).
- Remove the deepest-miss clause from the Fig. 3 caption (b), and get Fig. 3 regenerated without the in-image
  "deepest miss 0.0915 pu" label and the red triangles (d-figs; prose-free, new file + manifest). The .tex must not
  point at an image that prints a number the text no longer supports.
- Replace (c)+(d) with a limitation statement carrying: (i) the oracle is pandapower Newton-Raphson with
  `enforce_q_lims=True`, whose PV→PQ switching is one-way (a generator that hits a limit is not released when its
  voltage moves to the other side of setpoint); (ii) under a re-solve that releases wrongly pinned generators, the
  featured case is not a violation (value from N2; teammates' back-off gives 0.94507, equal to the case's own N-0
  minimum); (iii) the author found this, as with the earlier clip artifact (l.121) — framing as a second self-found
  artifact is supported, but it is the author's choice whether to say so.
- State at that point that all missed rates in the paper are measured against pandapower labels (tie to P-003).

**Branch 2 — N2 does not confirm** (N2's re-solve of the featured case stays below 0.94, fails to converge, or N2's
full-set flip share is negligible and the featured case is not among the flips):
- (c)/(d) still go: the mechanism must be rewritten from `data/miss_mechanism.json` (absorbing-limit pin, V below
  setpoint), not from the "collapse" reading.
- (a) and (b) may stay only with an explicit caveat that the value depends on the Q-limit convention: with Q limits
  off the same case solves to 0.9390 pu at a different bus (IEEE 20), a 0.0905 pu difference
  (`data/qlims_off_check.json`); and that two independent back-off re-solves (panel) gave 0.94507. Present the label
  as disputed between solvers, not as an established collapse.
- Fig. 3 annotation (e): keep only if the caption carries the same caveat; otherwise regenerate without it.

**Both branches:**
- Do not describe the event as "sudden collapse" or as something a pre-outage model "cannot foresee"; neither is
  supported by the data (the case is certified by both models: histgb@0.97 in seeds 2 and 3, and it is the maximum
  of the ridge pooled misses at 0.90 as well).
- The adjacent clause in (c)'s paragraph "whether or not the grid crashed in the first place, which is called
  dynamic stability" conflates static voltage collapse with dynamic stability (review PS minor; placement plan E9b).
  It sits in the same sentence cluster; fix in the same edit (content: the study does not check static voltage
  collapse proximity or dynamic/transient stability; name whichever the author means).
